How It Works
1
Select or drag a .pptx file from your computer.
2
Click Convert to Word. The local server processes everything on your machine — no data leaves your computer.
3
All slides are merged into a single continuous textbook-style document. Slide titles become section headings; bullets, tables, and images flow naturally without one-page-per-slide waste.
4
Your _textbook.docx file downloads automatically.
Prerequisites: start the local server first — python ppt_to_word_server.py — then open http://localhost:5000 in your browser.
