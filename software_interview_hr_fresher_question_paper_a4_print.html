Software Interview HR Questions - Fresher
Only Important and Repeated HR Questions for Fresher Software Interviews
Instructions: Prepare all questions thoroughly. Keep answers concise, professional, and example-based using your academics, projects, internships, certifications, and teamwork experiences.

Section A: Personal Introduction and Career Clarity

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. Walk me through your resume.
  3. Introduce yourself in 60 seconds.
  4. What kind of person are you outside academics?
  5. Why did you choose engineering/computer science/your degree?
  6. Why do you want to start your career in software?
  7. What inspired you to apply for this role?
  8. What are your short-term career goals?
  9. What are your long-term career goals?
  10. Where do you see yourself in 3 years?
  11. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
  12. What does success mean to you?
  13. What motivates you to work hard?
  14. How do you define professional growth?
  15. What do you expect from your first job?

Section B: Company Fit and Role Interest

  1. Why do you want to join our company?
  2. What do you know about our company?
  3. Why should we hire you?
  4. Why should we hire you as a fresher?
  5. What makes you different from other candidates?
  6. How will you add value in your first six months?
  7. Why this role and not another role?
  8. What are your expectations from this role?
  9. What type of work environment helps you perform best?
  10. Do you prefer individual work or team work? Why?
  11. How do you handle working under pressure?
  12. How do you handle strict deadlines?
  13. Are you willing to relocate?
  14. Are you open to rotational shifts?
  15. Do you have any questions for us?

Section C: Strengths, Weaknesses, and Behavioral Questions

  1. What are your strengths?
  2. Which strength helps you most in software projects?
  3. What is your biggest weakness?
  4. What weakness are you currently improving?
  5. Tell me about a failure and what you learned from it.
  6. Tell me about a challenging situation you faced.
  7. Describe a time when you made a mistake. How did you fix it?
  8. Describe a conflict in your team and how you handled it.
  9. Tell me about a time you took initiative.
  10. Tell me about a time you showed leadership.
  11. How do you respond to constructive criticism?
  12. How do you stay calm in stressful situations?
  13. How do you manage multiple tasks at the same time?
  14. How do you prioritize tasks when everything seems urgent?
  15. How do you ensure quality in your work?

Section D: Project, Learning Attitude, and Workplace Readiness

  1. Explain your final year project in simple terms.
  2. What was your exact role in your project?
  3. What problem were you trying to solve in your project?
  4. What challenges did you face in your project and how did you overcome them?
  5. What did you learn from your internship/project experience?
  6. Which technical skill did you learn recently and why?
  7. How do you keep yourself updated with new technologies?
  8. How do you learn a technology you have never used before?
  9. If assigned a task outside your comfort zone, how will you approach it?
  10. What would you do if your teammate is not contributing?
  11. What would you do if you disagree with your team lead?
  12. How do you communicate with non-technical team members?
  13. How do you handle repetitive tasks without losing focus?
  14. What are your salary expectations as a fresher?
  15. If selected, when can you join?
End of Paper
