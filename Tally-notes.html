Part A — Getting Started with Tally Prime

1. What is Tally Prime, and Why Learn It?

Tally Prime is accounting software used by businesses to record every money transaction, manage stock, calculate GST and TDS, run payroll, and instantly generate financial reports — replacing manual, paper-based bookkeeping. It is one of the most widely used accounting tools in India, and proficiency in it is a standard, often mandatory, requirement for Accounts Executive, Accounts Assistant, and Billing Executive jobs.

Job Relevance
  • Every entry made is called a "voucher" – the basic building block of all accounting in Tally.
  • Reports (Balance Sheet, GST returns, Outstanding lists) are generated automatically and instantly from the vouchers entered – never calculated by hand.
  • Real accounting jobs use Tally for day-to-day billing, GST filing support, bank reconciliation, salary processing, and month-end/year-end closing.

2. Downloading, Installing & Starting Tally Prime

Tally Prime must be installed once on the computer before use. After installation, it opens to a screen listing your companies, and then to the Gateway of Tally.

  • Download the Tally Prime setup file from the official Tally Solutions website.
  • Run the installer and follow the on-screen steps (Install for Single User is common for learning/practice).
  • Open Tally Prime from the desktop icon; the first screen lists any companies already created.
  • Tally Prime is almost entirely operated using the keyboard (function keys, Enter/Escape) rather than the mouse – this speed is exactly what employers expect from an experienced operator.

3. Gateway of Tally – Understanding the Screen

The Gateway of Tally is the main home screen from which every other screen is reached.

  • Top bar – shows the current company name and financial year.
  • Left menu – main options: Create Company, Vouchers, Reports.
  • Right-side button list – shows function-key shortcuts available on the current screen.
  • Press Esc repeatedly to go back a screen at a time; press Ctrl+M from anywhere to jump straight back to the Gateway of Tally.
  • Press Alt+G ("Go To") at any time to open a search box and jump directly to any report, voucher, or master by typing its name.

4. Creating a Company

A "Company" in Tally represents one business/organisation whose books of accounts you want to maintain. Every ledger, voucher, and report belongs to this one company.

  • From the startup screen, select Create Company.
  • Fill in Name, Mailing Address, State, Pin Code, and Financial Year start date (e.g. 1-Apr-2025).
  • Fill GSTIN/Company details now if known, or leave blank and enable later under Statutory Features.
  • Press Ctrl+A (or Enter through all fields) to save.
Path: Startup Screen -> Create Company

Name              : ABC Traders
Mailing Address   : 12, MG Road, Hyderabad
State             : Telangana
Pin Code          : 500001
Financial Year From: 1-Apr-2025
Books Beginning From: 1-Apr-2025

Press Ctrl+A to accept and save the company.

5. Loading, Selecting, Altering & Shutting a Company

In real office use, multiple client companies are often maintained in one Tally installation, so switching between them correctly is a daily skill.

  • Select a Company – press F1 (or K at the startup list) and choose from the list of existing companies to open it.
  • Alter Company details – press Alt+F3 from the Gateway of Tally to reach the company alteration menu (change name, address, financial year, etc.).
  • Shut a Company – press Alt+F1 to close the current company without closing Tally itself, e.g. before opening a different client's company.

6. Company Features (F11) & Configuration (F12)

These two screens turn Tally's optional modules on or off, so the software only shows the features that specific business needs.

  • F11 (Features) – company-specific switches: Accounting Features, Inventory Features, Statutory & Taxation (GST, TDS), Payroll. These affect only the current company.
  • F12 (Configure) – general display/behaviour settings (e.g. invoice printing options, extra fields shown), applying more broadly across reports and vouchers.
  • A beginner should enable: "Maintain Accounts" and "Integrate Accounts with Inventory" immediately, then GST/TDS/Payroll as each topic is reached.
Path: Gateway of Tally -> F11 (Company Features)

Maintain Accounts                : Yes
Enable Inventory                 : Yes
Integrate Accounts with Inventory: Yes
Enable Goods and Services Tax(GST): Yes  (turn on when starting the GST section)
Enable Tax Deducted at Source(TDS): Yes  (turn on when starting the TDS section)
Enable Payroll                   : Yes  (turn on when starting the Payroll section)

7. The Golden Rules of Accounting (Beginner Refresher)

Tally applies these traditional double-entry rules automatically behind the scenes every time a voucher is entered, so understanding them makes every entry make sense.

  • Personal Account (people, firms, banks): Debit the receiver, Credit the giver.
  • Real Account (assets, cash, stock): Debit what comes in, Credit what goes out.
  • Nominal Account (expenses, incomes): Debit all expenses and losses, Credit all incomes and gains.
  • In every voucher, total Debit must always equal total Credit – Tally will not let an unbalanced entry be saved.

8. Types of Accounts & Voucher Types – Quick Preview

Before going topic by topic, it helps to see the whole map: every transaction in a business falls into one of a small set of standard voucher types, each with its own function key.

  • Pure Accounting: Payment (F5), Receipt (F6), Contra (F4), Journal (F7).
  • Trading: Sales (F8), Purchase (F9), Debit Note, Credit Note, Sales/Purchase Order.
  • Inventory-only: Stock Journal, Physical Stock, Delivery Note, Receipt Note.
  • Payroll: Payroll Voucher (Ctrl+F4), Attendance Voucher.

Part B — Masters: Ledgers, Groups & Stock

9. Understanding Groups (Chart of Accounts)

A Group is a category that similar ledgers are organised under, so reports (like the Balance Sheet) automatically total them correctly. Tally Prime comes with 28 pre-defined Primary Groups already set up; a beginner rarely needs to create new groups, only ledgers under the existing ones.

  • Capital Account – owner's capital, reserves.
  • Sundry Debtors – customers who owe you money (assets).
  • Sundry Creditors – suppliers you owe money to (liabilities).
  • Cash-in-Hand / Bank Accounts – cash and bank balances.
  • Purchase Accounts / Sales Accounts – trading purchases and sales.
  • Direct/Indirect Expenses and Direct/Indirect Incomes – feed the Profit & Loss Account.
  • Fixed Assets – land, machinery, furniture (long-term assets).
  • Duties & Taxes – GST, TDS, and other statutory tax ledgers.

10. Creating a Single Ledger

A Ledger is an individual account – a specific customer, a specific expense like "Rent", or "Cash". Every voucher entry is made by selecting two or more ledgers. Tally starts every new company with two ready-made ledgers: Cash and Profit & Loss A/c.

  • Go to Create > Ledger, type a Name, then select the correct Group ("Under").
  • For customers/suppliers, set "Maintain balances bill-by-bill: Yes" so each invoice can be tracked and settled individually – essential for real Outstanding reports later.
  • Enter an Opening Balance if the ledger already has a balance on the first day of the books.
Path: Gateway of Tally -> Create -> Ledger

Name  : Ramesh Traders
Under : Sundry Debtors
Maintain balances bill-by-bill: Yes
Default Credit Period: 30 days
Opening Balance: 15,000  Dr

Press Ctrl+A to save.

11. Creating Multiple Ledgers Together

When many similar ledgers are needed (e.g. ten customer accounts), Tally offers a faster grid-style entry screen instead of creating them one at a time.

  • Go to Create > select "Multiple Ledgers" (or press Alt+G and search "Multi Ledger").
  • Choose the common "Under Group" for all of them (or "All Items" to set it per row).
  • Type each Name and Opening Balance directly into the grid rows.

12. Altering & Deleting Ledgers/Masters

  • Go to Alter > Ledger, select the ledger, make changes, press Ctrl+A to save.
  • To delete, open the ledger in Alter mode and press Alt+D. A ledger cannot be deleted if it has voucher entries against it – those entries must be removed first.

13. Stock Groups & Stock Categories

A Stock Group organises stock items by type (e.g. "Electronics", "Stationery"), similar to how a Group organises ledgers. A Stock Category is a second, independent way to classify items (e.g. by brand), used alongside Stock Groups for cross-analysis.

  • Go to Create > Stock Group, give it a Name, and select "Under" (or "Primary" for a top-level group).
  • Stock Categories are optional and created the same way via Create > Stock Category.

14. Units of Measure (Simple & Compound)

A Unit defines how a stock item is counted or measured – Numbers, Kilograms, Litres, Boxes, etc. A Compound Unit links two units together (e.g. 1 Box = 12 Pieces) so stock can be entered in either unit and converted automatically.

  • Go to Create > Unit, enter a Symbol (e.g. "Nos", "Kg") and Formal Name.
  • For a compound unit, select Type "Compound", then choose the First Unit and Second Unit with the conversion factor (e.g. 1 Box = 12 Pcs).

15. Creating a Stock Item

A Stock Item is the actual product or material being bought, sold, or manufactured – the inventory equivalent of a ledger.

  • Go to Create > Stock Item, enter a Name, select the Stock Group ("Under") and Unit.
  • Optionally set GST details (rate, HSN code) directly on the item, so every invoice using it auto-calculates tax correctly.
  • Enter an Opening Balance/Quantity/Rate if stock already exists on the first day of books.
Path: Gateway of Tally -> Create -> Stock Item

Name        : LED Bulb 9W
Under       : Electronics
Units       : Nos
GST Applicable: Applicable   HSN Code: 8539   Rate: 12%
Opening Balance: 100 Nos @ Rs.60 each

Press Ctrl+A to save.

16. Godowns / Locations

A Godown (or Location) represents a physical storage place – a warehouse, shop floor, or branch – so stock quantity can be tracked per location, not just as one company-wide total.

  • Enable in F11: "Maintain multiple Godowns? Yes".
  • Go to Create > Godown, enter a Name (e.g. "Main Warehouse", "Retail Shop").
  • Every inventory voucher then asks which Godown the stock is moving into or out of.

17. Price Levels & Price Lists

A real business often sells the same item at different rates to different types of customers – e.g. Wholesale vs Retail price. Price Levels define these customer categories, and a Price List sets the actual selling rate for each stock item at each level, so the correct rate fills in automatically during billing.

Common in Sales Jobs
  • Enable in F12 (Configure) or F11: "Use Multiple Price Levels? Yes".
  • Go to Create > Price Level, create levels such as "Wholesale" and "Retail".
  • Go to Create > Price List, select a Price Level, then set the rate for each stock item at that level.
  • When billing, select the customer's Price Level in the Sales Voucher, and Tally auto-fills the correct item rate.
Path: Gateway of Tally -> Create -> Price Level
Name: Wholesale
Name: Retail

Path: Gateway of Tally -> Create -> Price List -> Wholesale
Item: LED Bulb 9W    Rate: 55   (lower rate for bulk buyers)

Path: Gateway of Tally -> Create -> Price List -> Retail
Item: LED Bulb 9W    Rate: 70   (normal shop price)

18. Reorder Levels

A Reorder Level is the minimum stock quantity at which Tally will flag an item as needing to be restocked, helping avoid running out of fast-moving items – a common daily check for a store/inventory job.

  • Go to Gateway of Tally > Reorder Levels (under Inventory Info), select the Stock Group or Item.
  • Enter the Reorder Quantity and Minimum Order Quantity for each item.
  • View the result anytime in Display More Reports > Inventory Reports > Reorder Status, which lists items that have fallen below their set level.

19. Batch-wise Details & Expiry Dates

Batch tracking records stock in separate lots (batches), each with its own manufacturing and expiry date – essential in pharmaceutical, food, and FMCG businesses so old stock is sold first and expired stock is easily identified.

Pharma / FMCG Jobs
  • Enable in F11 (Inventory Features): "Maintain Batch-wise Details? Yes" and "Track Date of Manufacture/Expiry? Yes".
  • When creating/altering the Stock Item, set "Maintain in Batches: Yes".
  • Every purchase/sales voucher for that item will now ask for the Batch Name, Manufacturing Date, and Expiry Date.
  • View batch-wise stock and expiry status in Display More Reports > Inventory Reports > Batch Summary.

20. Bill of Materials (BOM)

A Bill of Materials defines the "recipe" of raw materials and components needed to manufacture one unit of a finished stock item – used by manufacturing businesses to plan and record production.

  • While creating/altering a (finished) Stock Item, enable "Set Components (BOM)? Yes".
  • List each raw material item and the quantity required to produce one unit of the finished item.
  • This BOM is then used automatically by the Manufacturing Journal (see Part D) to consume raw materials and produce finished stock.
Path: Alter -> Stock Item -> Wooden Chair -> Set Components (BOM)? Yes

Component (Raw Material)   Quantity (per 1 Chair)
Wood Plank                  4 Nos
Screws                      12 Nos
Varnish                     0.25 Ltr

Part C — Accounting Vouchers

21. Voucher Entry Basics

A Voucher is the single document used to record one transaction – every accounting entry in Tally, without exception, is entered as some type of voucher. Getting comfortable with the voucher screen layout is the single most important practical skill for a Tally job.

  • Open any voucher from Gateway of Tally > Vouchers, or press its function-key shortcut directly.
  • Sales and Purchase vouchers can be entered in two modes: Accounting Invoice (no stock items, just ledger amounts – used for service businesses) and Item Invoice (select actual stock items with quantity and rate – used for trading/product businesses). Switch between them using Ctrl+H (Change Mode) on the voucher screen.
  • If a ledger you need does not exist yet, you do not need to leave the voucher: type the new name and press Alt+C to create it instantly without losing your place.
  • Every voucher requires today's (or the entered) Date at the top – press F2 to change it if needed.
  • Press Ctrl+A at any point to accept/save the voucher.
  • Press Page Up / Page Down to move to the previous/next saved voucher of the same type – useful for quickly checking or correcting recent entries.

22. Payment Voucher (F5)

Records money going OUT of the business – e.g. paying an expense, paying a supplier, or withdrawing cash. Debits the expense/party account and credits the Cash or Bank account paid from.

  • Press F5, select the Account paid FROM (Cash or a Bank ledger).
  • Under Particulars, select the ledger being paid TO (e.g. an expense or a supplier), and enter the Amount.
  • Press Ctrl+A to save.
Command: F5 (Payment Voucher)
Account (paid from): Cash
Particulars: Rent Paid   Amount: 5,000
Press Ctrl+A to save.

23. Receipt Voucher (F6)

Records money coming IN to the business – e.g. receiving payment from a customer, or the owner introducing capital. Debits the Cash/Bank account receiving the money and credits the source account.

  • Press F6, select the Account money is received INTO (Cash or Bank).
  • Under Particulars, select the source ledger (e.g. a customer, or Capital Account), and enter the Amount.
Command: F6 (Receipt Voucher)
Account (received into): Cash
Particulars: Ramesh Traders   Amount: 15,000
Press Ctrl+A to save.

24. Contra Voucher (F4)

Records a transfer of money strictly between the company's own Cash and Bank accounts – e.g. depositing cash into the bank, or withdrawing cash from the bank. Both ledgers involved must be Cash or Bank type.

  • Press F4, select the account money is going INTO.
  • Select the account money is coming FROM, and enter the Amount.
Command: F4 (Contra Voucher)
Account (deposited into): HDFC Bank
Particulars (from): Cash   Amount: 20,000
Press Ctrl+A to save.

25. Journal Voucher (F7)

Used for adjustment entries that do not directly involve Cash or Bank – e.g. adjusting outstanding expenses, writing off a bad debt, transferring an amount between two ledgers, or recording depreciation. This is the most "manual" voucher, requiring the user to select both the Debit and Credit ledgers directly.

  • Press F7. Select the ledger to Debit and enter the amount, then the ledger to Credit – Tally will not let you save until Debit total = Credit total.
Command: F7 (Journal Voucher)
Dr  Depreciation Expense    2,000
    Cr  Furniture               2,000  (year-end depreciation adjustment)
Press Ctrl+A to save.

26. Sales Voucher (F8)

Records goods or services sold to a customer – the most frequently used voucher in a billing/sales job. Increases the customer's (Sundry Debtor's) balance and records Sales income.

  • Press F8. In Item Invoice mode, select the Party (customer), the Sales Ledger, then each Stock Item with Quantity and Rate – the Amount and GST (if applicable) calculate automatically.
  • In Accounting Invoice mode (for services, no stock), simply select the Party and the income ledger with the amount.
Command: F8 (Sales Voucher - Item Invoice mode)
Party A/c Name: Meera Book Depot
Sales Ledger: Sales Accounts
Item: Notebook   Qty: 40   Rate: 50   Amount: 2,000
Press Ctrl+A to save.

27. Purchase Voucher (F9)

Records goods or services bought from a supplier – the mirror opposite of the Sales voucher. Increases the supplier's (Sundry Creditor's) balance and records Purchase expense/stock coming in.

  • Press F9. Select the Party (supplier), Purchase Ledger, then each Stock Item with Quantity and Rate.
Command: F9 (Purchase Voucher - Item Invoice mode)
Party A/c Name: Kiran Stores
Purchase Ledger: Purchase Accounts
Item: Notebook   Qty: 100   Rate: 30   Amount: 3,000
Press Ctrl+A to save.

28. Debit Note & Credit Note

These record returns and post-invoice corrections. A Debit Note is raised when the business returns goods to a supplier (reducing what is owed to them) or needs to claim a reduction. A Credit Note is raised when a customer returns goods (reducing what they owe) or is given a price correction.

  • Enable in F11: "Use debit/credit notes? Yes".
  • Debit Note: press Ctrl+F9, select the supplier and the returned item/amount.
  • Credit Note: press Ctrl+F8, select the customer and the returned item/amount.
Command: Ctrl+F8 (Credit Note)
Party A/c Name: Meera Book Depot
Sales Ledger: Sales Accounts
Item: Notebook   Qty: 5   Rate: 50   Amount: 250   (5 defective notebooks returned)
Press Ctrl+A to save.

29. Sales Order & Purchase Order

Orders record an agreement to buy/sell in the future, before the actual delivery and invoice happen – they do NOT affect the accounts or stock balance, only track pending commitments.

  • Enable in F11: "Allow Purchase Order Processing? Yes" and "Allow Sales Order Processing? Yes".
  • Purchase Order: press Alt+F4. Sales Order: press Alt+F5. Enter the party and expected items/quantities.
  • When goods actually arrive/are delivered, the order can be recalled inside the Purchase/Sales voucher ("Order Details") so it auto-fills from the order and marks it fulfilled.
  • Track pending orders via Display More Reports > Statements of Inventory > Purchase/Sales Order Outstanding.

30. Voucher Numbering Methods & Voucher Class

Voucher Numbering controls how each voucher gets its serial number, which matters for audit and legal compliance. A Voucher Class is a template that automates repetitive ledger allocations (like GST or freight) within a voucher type, saving time on every single entry.

  • Go to Alter > Voucher Type > select a type (e.g. Sales) – set Numbering Method to Automatic (Tally assigns numbers), Manual (user types each number), or Automatic (Manual Override).
  • To create a Voucher Class: while altering a Voucher Type, enter a Class Name and define default ledgers (e.g. automatically add a "Round Off" or "Output CGST/SGST" ledger every time), so the biller doesn't need to add them manually on every invoice.
Path: Gateway of Tally -> Alter -> Voucher Type -> Sales
Method of Numbering: Automatic
Name of Class: GST Sales
   Default Accounting Allocation: Output CGST, Output SGST (auto-added on each invoice)

31. Discounts in Invoices (Trade Discount Column)

A Discount column can be added directly to the Item Invoice so a percentage or flat reduction is applied to each line item's rate before totalling – common in retail and wholesale billing.

Retail Billing
  • Enable in F12 (Configure) on the invoice screen: "Show column for Discount? Yes" (or enable "Use separate Discount column" in F11 Invoicing features).
  • While billing, simply type a discount percentage or amount in that column against the item; the item's Amount reduces automatically before the total.
Item: LED Bulb 9W   Qty: 20   Rate: 70   Discount: 10%   Amount: 1,260
(20 x 70 = 1,400, less 10% discount = 1,260)

32. Optional & Post-Dated Vouchers

An Optional voucher is saved for reference but does NOT affect any ledger balance or report until it is converted to a Regular voucher – useful for provisional/draft entries. A Post-Dated voucher is dated in the future and only takes effect in the books once that date arrives – useful for post-dated cheques.

  • While on any voucher screen, press Ctrl+L to mark it as Optional (or Alt+L in some versions), shown with "Optional" printed on the voucher.
  • To make a voucher Post-Dated, simply enter a future date in the Date field and confirm; Tally will ask to confirm it as post-dated.

Part D — Inventory Vouchers & Stock Management

33. Stock Journal

Used to record internal stock movements that are NOT a sale or purchase – e.g. transferring stock between godowns, or consuming raw material to produce a finished item.

Inventory
  • Press Alt+F7. Under "Source (Consumption)", list items/quantities going out; under "Destination (Production)", list items/quantities coming in.
Command: Alt+F7 (Stock Journal)
Source (Consumption): Raw Cotton   50 Kg
Destination (Production): Finished Cloth   45 Meters
Press Ctrl+A to save.

34. Physical Stock Voucher

Used to record the actual, physically counted stock quantity after a manual stock-take, so Tally's book quantity can be corrected to match reality – a routine monthly/annual task in any stock-handling job.

Inventory
  • Access via Alt+G (Go To) and type "Physical Stock", or Gateway of Tally > Vouchers > Physical Stock.
  • Enter the actual counted quantity for each item; any difference from the book quantity is called "Shortage" or "Excess" and shows in the Stock Summary until adjusted with a Stock Journal.

35. Delivery Note & Receipt Note

These are inventory-only vouchers used when goods physically move before the financial invoice is raised – common when dispatch and billing happen on different days. A Delivery Note records goods sent out to a customer; a Receipt Note records goods received in from a supplier.

Inventory
  • Delivery Note: press Alt+F8, select the customer and items dispatched.
  • Receipt Note: press Alt+F9, select the supplier and items received.
  • When the actual Sales/Purchase invoice is later raised, it can pull in ("Track") the Delivery/Receipt Note reference so quantities aren't entered twice.

36. Godown Transfer

Moves stock from one godown/location to another within the same company, without changing the total quantity owned – recorded using a Stock Journal with the same item as both source and destination, but different godowns.

Inventory
Command: Alt+F7 (Stock Journal)
Source: LED Bulb 9W   30 Nos   Godown: Main Warehouse
Destination: LED Bulb 9W   30 Nos   Godown: Retail Shop
Press Ctrl+A to save.

37. Manufacturing Journal

A special version of the Stock Journal used specifically to produce a finished item using its Bill of Materials (BOM, see Section 20) – raw materials are automatically consumed in the correct quantities the moment the finished quantity is entered.

Manufacturing
  • Open Stock Journal (Alt+F7) and change its type to "Manufacturing Journal" (select the finished item that has a BOM set).
  • Enter the quantity of the finished item to be produced; Tally auto-fills the raw materials needed based on the BOM.
Command: Alt+F7 (Manufacturing Journal)
Name of Product: Wooden Chair   Quantity: 10

Components (auto-filled from BOM, x10):
Wood Plank   40 Nos
Screws       120 Nos
Varnish      2.5 Ltr
Press Ctrl+A to save. (Raw materials reduce, 10 Wooden Chairs are added to stock)

38. POS (Point of Sale) Invoice

A simplified, fast billing screen designed for retail counter sales – walk-in customers, cash sales, immediate printing – without needing to select a specific customer ledger for every single sale.

Retail Jobs
  • Enable in F11: "Enable POS Invoicing? Yes", then create a POS Voucher Type (Gateway of Tally > Create > Voucher Type, Type = Sales, tick "Use for POS Invoicing").
  • Open the POS Invoice screen via Alt+G, select items and quantities; payment mode (Cash/Card/UPI) is chosen at the end.
  • Prints a compact receipt-style bill suited to a counter printer.

39. Stock Valuation Methods (FIFO, LIFO, Average Cost)

When the same item is purchased at different rates over time, Tally needs a rule to decide which cost to use when it is sold – this rule is the Stock Valuation Method, and it directly affects the Gross Profit shown in reports.

Costing / Inventory Jobs
  • FIFO (First-In-First-Out) – assumes the oldest purchased stock is sold first; closing stock is valued at the most recent purchase rates.
  • LIFO (Last-In-First-Out) – assumes the most recently purchased stock is sold first (less commonly used in India for tax reasons).
  • Average Cost – values every unit at the weighted average rate of all purchases.
  • Set this per stock item: Alter > Stock Item > select the Costing Method (under "Set/Modify Standard Rates" or Valuation Method field).
Example: Item "Steel Rod" purchased twice:
  Lot 1: 100 units @ Rs.50 = 5,000
  Lot 2: 100 units @ Rs.60 = 6,000
  Total 200 units, Total cost 11,000

FIFO: selling 120 units uses all of Lot 1 (100 @ 50) + 20 of Lot 2 (20 @ 60) = 6,200 cost
Average Cost: 11,000 / 200 = Rs.55 average; 120 units = 6,600 cost

40. Godown-wise Stock & Movement Analysis

These reports break down stock quantity and movement history by location, letting a warehouse or store job holder see exactly what is where, and how fast each item is moving.

Inventory
  • Godown-wise Stock – Display More Reports > Inventory Reports > Stock Summary > press Alt+G to view "Godown-wise" breakup.
  • Movement Analysis – Display More Reports > Inventory Reports > Movement Analysis, shows every inward/outward transaction for an item, useful for tracing discrepancies.
  • Stock Ageing Analysis – shows how long each batch/quantity has been sitting unsold, helping identify slow-moving or dead stock.

Part E — Bills Management & Outstanding

41. Bill-wise Details in Depth

When "Maintain balances bill-by-bill" is set Yes on a party ledger, Tally tracks each individual invoice separately instead of just one running total – this is essential in real jobs so it is always clear exactly which invoice a payment is settling.

Daily Job Task
  • New Ref – used on a Sales/Purchase voucher to create a new, named bill reference (e.g. "Inv-101") that can be tracked and settled later.
  • Against Ref – used on a Receipt/Payment voucher to settle (close) an existing, specific bill reference by selecting it from the list.
  • Advance – used when money is received/paid before any invoice exists yet (e.g. an advance from a customer).
  • On Account – used when a payment/receipt is not linked to any specific bill at all, left as a general balance against the party.
Sales Voucher (F8) to Meera Book Depot, Amount 2,000:
  Bill-wise Details: New Ref -> Name: Inv-101 -> Amount: 2,000

Later, Receipt Voucher (F6) when she pays:
  Account: Cash   Particulars: Meera Book Depot   Amount: 2,000
  Bill-wise Details: Against Ref -> Inv-101 -> Amount: 2,000  (fully settles that invoice)

42. Outstanding Receivables & Payables Reports

These are among the most frequently checked reports in any accounts job – they list exactly who owes the business money (Receivables) and who the business owes money to (Payables), invoice by invoice, with how many days overdue.

Most-Used Report
  • Go to Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Statements of Accounts > Outstanding > Receivables (for customers) or Payables (for suppliers).
  • Each row shows the party, the specific pending bill reference, its date, due date, and overdue days – used daily to decide who to follow up for payment.
  • Press Alt+F1 on this report to see a detailed, bill-by-bill breakup instead of just the party total.

43. Interest Calculation on Outstanding Bills

Tally can automatically calculate interest owed on overdue bills based on a set annual rate, saving the manual calculation a business would otherwise do for late-paying customers.

  • Enable in F11: "Activate Interest Calculation? Yes".
  • On the party ledger (Alter > Ledger), set "Activate Interest Calculation? Yes", enter the annual Rate (e.g. 18%) and the calculation basis (e.g. on Bill-by-Bill balances).
  • View the result in Display More Reports > Statements of Accounts > Interest Calculations, which shows interest accrued on each overdue bill.
Path: Alter -> Ledger -> Meera Book Depot
Activate Interest Calculation? Yes
Rate: 18% per annum   Style: Calculate on Bill-by-Bill balances

Part F — GST (Goods and Services Tax)

44. What is GST? – Quick Basics

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is India's indirect tax charged on the sale of goods and services. Handling GST correctly in Tally is one of the most commonly tested skills in Accounts job interviews.

GST
  • CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) – charged together, in equal halves, on sales WITHIN the same state.
  • IGST (Integrated GST) – charged as one single tax on sales BETWEEN different states (inter-state).
  • HSN Code – a standard numeric code classifying each product, required on GST invoices.
  • Input GST (paid on purchases) can generally be claimed as a credit against Output GST (collected on sales), reducing the net tax payable.

45. Enabling GST in Tally Prime

GST
  • Go to F11 > Statutory & Taxation > "Enable Goods and Services Tax (GST)? Yes".
  • Enter the company's State (determines whether a sale is intra-state or inter-state) and GSTIN (the 15-digit GST registration number).
Path: Gateway of Tally -> F11 -> Statutory & Taxation
Enable GST? Yes
State: Telangana
GSTIN/UIN: 36ABCDE1234F1Z5
Press Ctrl+A to save.

46. Creating GST Tax Ledgers

  • Tally then auto-calculates and adds these on every applicable invoice, based on the GST rate set on the stock item and whether the sale is intra- or inter-state.

49. GST Reports – GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

GST

  • GSTR-3B – a summary monthly return showing total tax liability and input credit, used to calculate the actual GST payment due.

50. e-Way Bill & e-Invoice Basics

Two mandatory digital GST compliance requirements for many businesses, both directly generated from within Tally – a genuinely current, real-world skill expected in GST-related job roles.

GSTCurrent Compliance
  • e-Way Bill – a mandatory document for transporting goods above a value threshold, containing item, vehicle, and route details. Enable in F11 > GST Details > "e-Way Bill applicable? Yes"; Tally then prompts for transport details on qualifying invoices and can export the JSON to upload on the e-Way Bill portal (or generate directly if API-integrated).

Part G — TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)

51. What is TDS? – Basics

TDS means that when a business pays certain kinds of expenses (like professional fees, rent, or contractor payments) above a threshold, it must deduct a small percentage as tax right at the time of payment and deposit it with the government on the payee's behalf, rather than paying the government separately later.

TDSCommon Interview Topic
  • The person/business making the payment is called the Deductor; the one receiving payment (with tax already deducted) is the Deductee.
  • Each expense type has its own "Nature of Payment" and TDS rate, e.g. Professional Fees (Section 194J) at 10%, Rent (Section 194I) at 10%, Contractor Payments (Section 194C) at 1%/2%.
  • The deducted amount is paid to the government using a Challan, and a TDS Certificate (Form 16A) is later issued to the deductee as proof.

52. Enabling TDS & Creating TDS Ledgers

TDS
  • Enable in F11 > Statutory & Taxation: "Enable Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)? Yes"; enter the company's TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number).
  • Create a TDS duty ledger, e.g. "TDS on Professional Fees", Under "Duties & Taxes", Type of Duty/Tax = TDS, and select its Nature of Payment.
  • On the expense ledger being paid (e.g. "Professional Fees"), and on the party ledger being paid, set "Is TDS Deductable? Yes" and select the applicable Nature of Payment.
Path: F11 -> Statutory & Taxation -> Enable TDS? Yes -> TAN: HYDA12345B

Create -> Ledger -> Name: TDS on Professional Charges
Under: Duties & Taxes   Type of Duty/Tax: TDS
Nature of Payment: Fees for Professional Services (Section 194J)

Alter -> Ledger -> Professional Fees (expense ledger)
Is TDS Deductable? Yes   Nature of Payment: Fees for Professional Services

53. Recording a TDS Transaction

TDS

When paying an eligible expense, the TDS amount is deducted directly within the same voucher, so only the net amount actually leaves the Cash/Bank account.

  • Use a Payment or Journal voucher, debit the expense for the full amount, then include the TDS ledger for the deducted amount, and credit Bank/Cash for the balancing net amount.
Example: Professional Fees bill of Rs.20,000, TDS @10% = Rs.2,000

Command: F7 (Journal Voucher)
Dr  Professional Fees              20,000
    Cr  TDS on Professional Charges          2,000
    Cr  CA Consultants (party)               18,000
Press Ctrl+A to save.

Later, pay the TDS to the government (Payment Voucher, F5):

Part H — Financial Reports

55. Trial Balance

A list of every ledger's closing balance, split into Debit and Credit columns – the first check performed before preparing final accounts, since total Debits must always equal total Credits.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Trial Balance.
  • Press Alt+F1 to expand every group down to individual ledgers.

56. Profit & Loss Account

Shows whether the business made a profit or loss over a period, by comparing total Income against total Expenses.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Profit & Loss A/c.
  • Automatically updates in real time as vouchers are entered – no manual calculation needed.
  • Press Alt+F2 to change the period it is calculated over (e.g. month, quarter, full year).

57. Balance Sheet

Shows the financial position of the business on a given date – what it Owns (Assets) versus what it Owes (Liabilities) and the Owner's Capital, which must always balance (Assets = Liabilities + Capital).

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Balance Sheet.
  • The Profit/Loss figure from the P&L Account automatically carries into the Balance Sheet under Capital.

58. Day Book

A chronological list of every voucher entered on a given day (or period) – the fastest way to review or audit everything that was recorded.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Day Book (or press Alt+G and type "Day Book").
  • Press F2 to jump to a different date, or Alt+F2 to view a date range.

59. Ledger Report (Ledger Voucher Display)

Shows every voucher entry affecting one specific ledger, in date order, with a running balance – used constantly to check "what does this customer/supplier/expense actually show".

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Account Books > Ledger, then select the ledger.

60. Stock Summary

Shows the closing quantity and value of every stock item, grouped by Stock Group – the inventory equivalent of the Trial Balance.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Stock Summary.
  • Drill down (press Enter) into any group to see individual item balances, and further into an item to see its full voucher history.

61. Cash Book & Bank Book

Specialised ledger reports showing only Cash or only Bank transactions, in date order with a running balance – used daily to verify how much cash/bank balance the business actually has.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Account Books > Cash/Bank Book, select the Cash or Bank ledger.

62. Cash Flow & Funds Flow Statement

The Cash Flow Statement shows how cash moved in and out of the business over a period, split into Operating, Investing, and Financing activities. The Funds Flow Statement shows how working capital changed between two Balance Sheet dates.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Cash Flow Statement / Funds Flow Statement.
  • Both are generated automatically from the same voucher data already entered – no separate data entry needed.

63. Ratio Analysis

Automatically calculates key financial ratios from the current books – e.g. Current Ratio, Debtors Turnover, Net Profit %. Useful for quickly assessing business health without manual calculation.

  • Gateway of Tally > Display More Reports > Ratio Analysis.

Part I — Banking & Other Features

64. Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)

Matches the bank balance as per the company's books against the balance shown on the actual bank statement, so any difference (e.g. a cheque issued but not yet cleared) is identified – a routine monthly task in almost every accounts job.

Monthly Job Task
  • Open the Bank ledger > press F5 (Bank Reconciliation) on the ledger display screen.
  • Against each transaction, enter the actual date it cleared in the bank statement.
  • Tally shows the reconciled balance and lists any still-unreconciled entries.

65. Cheque Printing

Prints a formatted cheque directly from a Payment voucher, matching the layout of the actual bank cheque book, avoiding handwritten cheques.

  • Set up the Bank ledger's cheque book details first (Alter > Bank Ledger > Cheque Books).
  • From a saved Payment voucher, press Alt+P (Print), then choose "Cheque" as the print option.

66. Backup and Restore Company Data

Backup creates a safe copy of the company data outside Tally, protecting against data loss; Restore brings that backup data back into Tally, e.g. on a new computer.

  • Backup: Gateway of Tally > (Company Info menu) > Backup > select the company and a destination folder.
  • Restore: same menu > Restore > select the backup source folder and destination.
  • A regular backup routine (e.g. daily or weekly) is standard professional practice in any accounts role.

67. Security Control (Users & Passwords) & Tally Vault

Security Control lets an administrator create multiple user logins with different permission levels (e.g. a data-entry operator who cannot view reports, versus a full administrator). Tally Vault is a separate, stronger encryption password that scrambles the entire company data file itself.

  • Enable in F11: "Enable Security Control? Yes", then create Security Levels and Users (with usernames/passwords) under Company Alteration.
  • Tally Vault Password is set at the time of Company Creation (or Alter Company); once set, the data cannot be opened at all without it – even Tally itself cannot recover a forgotten Vault password, so it must be noted safely.

Part K — Advanced Features

73. Cost Centres & Cost Categories

A Cost Centre tracks income/expense against a department, project, or branch, separate from the ledger it is posted to. Cost Categories group multiple parallel sets of cost centres.

  • Enable in F11: "Maintain Cost Centres? Yes"; create Cost Centres via Create > Cost Centre.
  • Every relevant voucher then asks which Cost Centre to allocate the amount against.
  • View totals per cost centre in Display More Reports > Statements of Accounts > Cost Centres.

74. Budgets & Scenarios

A Budget sets a planned spending/income limit for a ledger or group, so actual figures can be compared against it. A Scenario allows "what-if" reporting using provisional vouchers that don't affect the real books.

  • Create > Budget, set the period and budgeted amounts against Groups/Ledgers.
  • Compare via Display More Reports > Statements of Accounts > Budget Variance.

75. Multi-Currency

Allows recording transactions in a foreign currency while Tally automatically converts and reports them in the base currency using an exchange rate – essential for import/export businesses.

  • Enable in F11: "Enable Multi-Currency? Yes"; create Currency masters (Create > Currency) with their exchange rate.

76. Data Export, Import & Voucher Printing

  • Export – press Alt+E on any report/voucher, choose a format (Excel, PDF, XML, JSON).
  • Import – Gateway of Tally > Import Data, to bring in master/voucher data from another company or file.
  • Print – press Alt+P from any open voucher or report.
